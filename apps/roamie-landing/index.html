Your trip, one app — even when the signal disappears

Roamie brings itinerary planning, interactive maps, local picks, language help, an on-device travel assistant, and optional connections with other travelers into a single light, offline-friendly experience.

Built for real travel

Everything you need before and during a trip, aligned with how people actually move through cities and airports.

Offline-first maps & places

Explore with pins, categories, and context for your destination. Core map experiences stay usable when connectivity drops.

Itinerary that travels with you

Plan by day, reorder stops, and keep the schedule you care about in your pocket — synced when you are back online.

Curated local recommendations

Discover food, sights, and practical spots tailored to your trip instead of generic search noise.

Language cheat sheet

Quick phrases and references so ordering, directions, and emergencies feel less stressful abroad.

On-device travel assistant

Ask questions and brainstorm plans with an assistant that runs on your phone — no round-trips to a cloud model required for core use.

Connect with travelers

Optional social layer to meet people at your destination, message, and coordinate when you want company.
